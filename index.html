My name is Caleb Holcomb. I grew up in a remote Alaskan village populated by a few hundred people. Insomnia runs in the men in my family. I have been a commercial fisherman, a cashier, a student, a pizza chef, a blinds installer, a delivery driver, a substitute teacher, a construction worker, and an oneironaut. I am the eldest of 7 biological siblings and 8 stepsiblings. Rachel Dolezal is my aunt and I was born at her house. I almost lost the use my left hand at 17 due to severe burns. I have never seen the moon. One of these is a lie.

My debut project, Lullabies & Other Lies, is available everywhere.
